-The hyperparameters have a huge influence on the performance. The best example ist the hyperparameter epsilon. 
 Running our agent in the same environment with different epsilon leads to extraordinary performance differences.
 (plot)
-The learning rate impacts how fast the information of the agent is updated by interacting with its environment.
 In our case it had less influence then f. e. epsilon as long as we stayed in [0.2,0.6]. Leaving this intervall 
 led to drops of performance. 
-The best epsilon is highly dependent on the environment. In our example case the best value was ≈0.35.
